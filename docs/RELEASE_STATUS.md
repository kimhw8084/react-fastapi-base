## Evidence policy

Reports under `evidence/current/` are generated from the current source and carry commit, timestamp, command, exit code, environment and hashes. Historical reports remain under historical directories and are not release proof. Local success does not certify company infrastructure, SEMI compliance, WCAG conformance, or production safety.

The deterministic RC.17 repository readiness report is `evidence/current/release/rc17-readiness-matrix.json`; its companion manifest and binding are `rc17-manifest.json` and `rc17-evidence-binding.json`. RC.16 and earlier corresponding artifacts remain immutable historical evidence. The source-bound independent deployment qualification is `evidence/current/deployment/qualification.json`; the source-bound storage qualification is `evidence/current/storage/qualification.json`; the reusable-platform aggregate is `evidence/current/reuse/qualification.json`. Canonical performance evidence is under `evidence/current/performance/`, including `qualification.json` and `browser.json`; all are repository evidence, not an operator-approved qualification file.
The repository identity is generated from the passing current source verification report by `scripts/generate_release_identity.py` and is intentionally outside the executable-source hash set to avoid a digest cycle. Its `verified_source_commit` and `source_digest` are the canonical executable-source identity. Evidence-only checkout/candidate heads are recorded separately and do not replace that identity.
